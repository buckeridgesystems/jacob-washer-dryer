About Purely Global

We rent used washers and dryers to people in Colorado Springs.

Our story

Purely Global started the way a lot of good ideas do: in a garage, with a truck and a simple belief that every household deserves clean clothes without the stress of a big upfront purchase. We saw neighbors stuck hauling laundry to the laundromat, renters who didn’t want to buy appliances they’d have to leave behind, and families whose machines broke at the worst possible time. So we started picking up dependable washers and dryers from trusted brands like Whirlpool, Kenmore, GE, and Amana, cleaning and testing each one until it ran like it should, and delivering them right to people’s doors. What began as a few machines lined up in our garage has grown into a rental service built on honest pricing, quick delivery, and real people who answer when you call. When you rent from us, you’re supporting a local business that cares about your home as much as you do, and we’re grateful to be part of your everyday routine.
