Questions about renting a washer and dryer

Here is what people ask us most. If your question isn't here, text us and ask.

Common questions

How much does a rental cost?

A used washer and dryer set is $50 a month. One machine, a washer or a dryer, is $35 a month.

What's the minimum?

4 months. Need it for less time? Text us to ask about shorter rentals.

What's the deposit?

$50, due at installation. We return it at the end of your rental if there's no major damage to the machines.

How do I pay?

We send you an invoice through Stripe each month, by email or text.

Do I sign a contract?

Yes, at installation. Want to read it first? Ask, and we'll send you a digital copy.

Is delivery and installation included?

Yes. We drop it off and hook it up for free in Colorado Springs.

How soon can I get a washer and dryer?

We install between 5 and 7 PM, as early as Monday. Text us your name and address to set it up.

Do you serve my area?

We serve Colorado Springs only. Not sure if your address is in the city? Text it to us and we'll check.

What should I put in my text?

Your name and your address. Tell us if you need a washer, a dryer, or both.
