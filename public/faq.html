Questions about renting a washer and dryer

Here is what people ask us most. If your question isn't here, text us and ask.

Common questions

How much does a rental cost?

A used washer and dryer set is $50 a month. One machine, a washer or a dryer, is $35 a month. There is no big upfront cost.

Is delivery and installation included?

In Colorado Springs, yes. We drop it off and hook it up for free. Outside the city, delivery is $50.

Do I need good credit or a contract?

No credit check and no long-term commitment. It's month to month, so you can cancel anytime.

How soon can I get a washer and dryer?

Delivery and installation are usually done the same week. Text us your name and address and we'll set it up.

Do you serve my area?

We serve Colorado Springs and the surrounding area. Not sure if you're covered? Text us your address.

What if a machine has a problem?

Maintenance is free. We maintain the machines we rent.

What should I put in my text?

Your name and your address. Tell us if you need a washer, a dryer, or both.
